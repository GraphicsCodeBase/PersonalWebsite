# Video — Cute Room Portfolio (Blender + Three.js)

## What it is
A beginner course that builds exactly the kind of thing I want: a stylised 3D room portfolio you explore in the browser. Blender for the scene, Three.js for the web side.

## Why it matters
It's a proven, finishable path. Following it means I'm adapting a working build rather than inventing the whole pipeline myself.

## The pattern it teaches (the important takeaways)
1. **Model simple.** Boxy low-poly furniture — no sculpting, no realistic modelling.
2. **Bake the lighting into textures** in Blender. The scene then needs almost no realtime lighting, which is why these sites are fast *and* pretty.
3. **Export as .glb** and load it in Three.js.
4. **Raycast clicks** on named objects to trigger content/animations.
5. Camera controls + a loading screen + hover states do most of the "feel".
