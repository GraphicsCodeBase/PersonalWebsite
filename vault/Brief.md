## What does the visitor do in 40 seconds?
1. Understand who I am
2. See ~3 projects worth looking at
3. Know how to contact me / find my GitHub

## What "v1 done" means
Two layers, both real:
1. **The content layer** - live URL, about, 3 projects, contact. Works everywhere, no 3D. This is the floor.
2. **The room layer** - one stylised room you can orbit and click, which opens that same content. This is the point.

If only layer 1 exists, I still have a personal website. That is deliberate. -> [[Roadmap]]

## Explicit non-goals for v1

- Comments, accounts, or any login
- CMS / admin panel
- Perfect design
- A full multi-room walkable house (that is v3, not v1)
